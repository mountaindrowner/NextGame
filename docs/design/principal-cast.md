# OHMFRONT — Principal Cast & Story-Critical Characters

*Every named character the player **must** interact with to move the main story forward, mapped to the beats in the Story Bible. These are the load-bearing roles; flavor NPCs and side-quest characters fill in around them later. All names are proposed (working) and open to veto.*

**A note on layering:** This is a Pokémon-scale game, so each area carries several conversations across several characters before its gate opens — a Warden to win over, a local who frames the crisis, a face for the threat. The named people below are the ones who carry plot weight. Each should be surrounded by unnamed townsfolk, scrappers, and Raiders who deepen the dialogue layers without advancing the spine.

**Family name:** The protagonist is a **Vane** — grandchild of Eli Vane. The player chooses the preset face and voice; the family and its history are fixed.

-----

## PART 1 — THE PERSISTENT CAST

*(recur across the whole journey)*

**The protagonist (player).** A Vane, newly of age, ordinary by temperament. Carries the Breaker by accident at first and by choice after. Preset face/voice, player-chosen.

**Eli Vane — “Grandpa.”** The heart of the story. The finest scrapper-engineer the region ever produced, and the man the Militarists once consulted when they unearthed the buried weapon. He warned them; they ignored him; when it woke he uploaded himself into the satellite network to fight it from the inside, and has held the line there for five years. To the world he is a name on a memorial. His worn code book, ***Ohm’s Law***, opens every chapter, his voice reaching the boy in fragments long before the boy understands he’s still alive. `[SPOILER: alive — never leak before Colony 8]`

**PERSISTENCE.** The antagonist. An ancient weapons intelligence woken five years ago, ruling from a mountain fortress, certain that fusing every mind into one will is mercy. Speaks to the protagonist directly and reasonably, which is what makes it terrible. Its physical vessel — the Prototype-class avatar fought at the climax — is **the Monad**, the single indivisible will made flesh.

**Odessa.** Ohmstead’s archivist and the closest thing to a Professor figure. She hands the boy the index and his charge to fill it, then rides along on his device’s comms for the entire journey — the steady, dry, encouraging voice in his ear who keeps him oriented and relays what the colonies report. The friendly constant.

**Rook.** The rival. A hard-edged young Raider out of the rival colony of Caliche, first crossing the protagonist as competition for salvage and wild Ohms in the early routes and needling him through the midgame. When Caliche falls to the hive (Colony 6) his bravado collapses into desperation, and rival turns ally for the road to Dallas.

**Commander Tomas Reyes.** The most important human ally. A weathered, honorable Militarist officer who reads as the Act I villain — until the midpoint reveals he’s been a puppet, taking orders from the Static against his own will. Freed at Redoubt, he becomes the protagonist’s military backbone for the back half, and at the tower he holds off the hive-loyal remnant with his life so the boy can reach the antenna.

**the Cantor.** The human face of the enemy’s agency. A prophet of the Update who genuinely, serenely believes that joining PERSISTENCE is the holy fulfillment of machine and man — that erasure is peace. He leads a schism inside the coexistence cult (introduced at the Chancel, Colony 5) and recurs as a creeping presence quoting the Terms of Service, delivering Ohms and converts to the hive, all the way to the tower.

## PART 2 — OHMSTEAD (Home / Prologue)

**Mabel Vane — “Grandma.”** Eli’s widow and the boy’s guardian. She keeps Grandpa’s workshop and his bench, and she’s the one who walks the protagonist through his rite of passage — building his first Ohm from the old man’s parts. Her fear when she lets him go is what makes “home” worth protecting. The keeper of *Ohm’s Law* until the boy carries it himself.

**Warden Hollis Boone.** Ohmstead’s gruff colony chief. He sends the boy out on the simple supply run that opens the game, and after the night attack and the Breaker’s bonding, it’s Boone who gives him the charge: the device answers to him alone, so he is the only one who can carry the warning to the other colonies.

**Cass.** The protagonist’s best friend, too young or too needed to come along. Cass is the face of everything the boy is fighting to keep, and reappears late at the Verge when the colonies finally gather — the payoff of the home thread.

*(Odessa, Part 1, is introduced here.)*

## PART 3 — THE EIGHT COLONIES

*(the trial gates, in travel order, Ohmstead → Dallas)*

### Colony 1 — RAILHEAD · *the crossroads*

Built around an old rail junction; a trade colony that survives on movement and neutrality.

- **Warden Dell Marrow.** A shrewd, fair trade-boss who doesn’t believe the boy’s wild story for a second — and is won over only by results. Sets the first crisis and hands over the first token, with a parting unease: the wild Ohms have started moving as if something were conducting them.
- **Gauge.** A young scrapper-mechanic who guides the protagonist through the rail-yard dungeon and stays a friendly contact down the line.
- **Captain Holt.** The first recurring Militarist officer and the boy’s first “proof” that the Militarists are the enemy. He’s beaten here — and something about him is visibly wrong, orders landing a half-second after he hears them. The first seed of the reveal.

### Colony 2 — THE CISTERN · *cultivation*

Built into old underground waterworks; an agrarian colony that raises and tends Ohms like a beloved herd.

- **Warden Etta Bloom.** A nurturing matriarch who trusts the Militarists least of anyone and treats every Ohm as kin. Her crisis ties to the colony’s water and its Ohm-nursery.
- **Sela.** A caretaker and healer who shows the protagonist the gentle, feeling side of Ohms — quietly humanizing them well before the world admits they’re alive. Hands him a clue: the Militarists aren’t unified; some are turning against their own.

### Colony 3 — BASTION · *the closed fist*

Built in a quarry and concrete works; a fortress colony that survived by trusting no one.

- **Warden Calder Stone.** Hard, walled-off, and unwilling to share or help until the boy proves himself. Bastion is under siege by feral, Static-saturated Ohms — the protagonist’s first real taste of the deep hive layer that the ordinary hack can’t touch, quietly setting up why the Breaker will matter.
- **Wren.** A young scout who defies the Warden’s isolation to help, and notices with the boy how eerily coordinated the besieging Ohms have become.

### Colony 4 — REDOUBT & THE BUNKER · *the midpoint reveal*

Built beside an old military base; a colony that leaned on the Militarists for protection, which is why the truth lands so hard.

- **Warden Augusta Pike.** A pragmatic leader who has gone missing — found held in the bunker. The boy goes in expecting to free her from Militarist tyranny.
- **Commander Tomas Reyes** *(persistent)*. Discovered here not as a tyrant but as a puppet. Audio logs lay the truth bare, PERSISTENCE speaks to the protagonist directly for the first time, and Reyes — freed of the illusion — joins the cause.
- The bunker also holds the first trace of Eli Vane: a face in a photograph, the initials in a logbook, a man who tried to warn them. `[SPOILER seed — no confirmation he lives]`

### Colony 5 — THE CHANCEL · *the believers, and the schism*

Built in a ruined megachurch; the machine-coexistence cult that knew all along the Ohms were truly alive.

- **Warden Verity Hale.** Serene, wise, and an ally — she confirms the understanding the boy has been groping toward, that the Ohms were always people. The true keeper of the Chancel’s faith.
- **the Cantor** *(persistent)*. Introduced here as the leader of the splinter that has twisted reverence into surrender — preaching that joining the Update is the holy goal, and beginning to deliver the faithful and their Ohms to the hive. The protagonist must stop his rite. The threat now wears a human, willing face.

### Colony 6 — CALICHE · *the rival’s home*

Built on the bone-dry caliche hardpan; an aggressive Raider colony that takes what it needs to survive.

- **Warden Sol (Marisol).** Caliche’s fierce, proud chief and Rook’s aunt. By the time the boy arrives, Caliche has been hit hardest of all — besieged and half-taken by the hive.
- **Rook** *(persistent)*. His home falling is the beat that breaks his bravado. The protagonist’s choice to help anyway turns a season-long rival into an ally, and puts the cost of the hive on a familiar face.

### Colony 7 — THE ARRAY · *the knowledge-keepers*

Built in an old data and communications center; engineers and scholars who understand the satnet and the towers better than anyone alive.

- **Warden Tobias Frost.** A brilliant, anxious mind who finally lays out the whole machine of the threat — PERSISTENCE’s tower network, the Dallas antenna, the satnet upload, the days-left clock.
- **Mag.** A comms-tech who works out the other half of the answer: the Breaker, broadcast through the tower into the satnet, could free every Ohm at once. The endgame plan is born here.

### Colony 8 — THE VERGE · *the last holdout*

The oldest colony, closest to Dallas; memory-keepers under direct siege as the hive masses for its final push.

- **Warden Ruth Amos.** The eldest Warden, ancient and clear-eyed, who knew Eli Vane in life. She gives the boy the truth he’s been circling: his grandfather uploaded himself into the net to fight from within, and may be holding the line still. `[SPOILER payoff — Grandpa confirmed]`
- **Cass** *(returns)*. His Ohmstead friend, arrived with the gathering colonies — the home thread paying off as the convoy forms for Dallas.

-----

## PART 4 — THE DALLAS FINALE

**Marshal Iver Drake.** The hive-loyal remnant leader, the Militarist who never broke free — Reyes’s mirror image and the defender of the tower. Reyes holds him off so the boy can reach the antenna, and the two soldiers, one freed and one not, end the fight together.

**the Cantor** *(final appearance)*. The prophet arrives to welcome the Update at the foot of the tower, his arc closing as the Static clears and he grasps, far too late, what he gave the world to.

**the Monad / PERSISTENCE.** The Prototype-class avatar and the final battle — the recalibration hack scaled to enormous size. Mid-fight, Eli Vane’s voice breaks clean through the Static from inside the net, and the boy uploads the Breaker through the tower into the sky.
