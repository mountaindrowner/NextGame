# OHMFRONT — Variable NPC System & Generic Cast

*The reusable, stampable NPCs that fill a Pokémon-scale world — the Bug-Catcher-Boy / Militant-A layer. These are templates, not individuals: define each once, then place it hundreds of times. Companion to the Principal Cast doc; feeds the Phase 2 bible. All names/classes are proposed (working).*

-----

## 1. HOW THE GENRE DOES IT (the model we’re copying)

In Pokémon, every generic person you fight belongs to a **trainer class** — a template that bundles five things:

1. **A sprite** (with gendered or palette variants).
1. **A team archetype** tied to a type — Bug Catchers run Bug-types, Fishermen run Water, Hikers run Rock/Ground.
1. **A habitat** — the class only appears where it makes sense (Bug Catchers in forests, Swimmers in water, Hikers in caves).
1. **A skill tier** — Youngsters/Lasses are the weak “first trainers”; Ace Trainers are balanced and strong.
1. **A voice** — one or two pre/post-battle lines that give cheap personality.

The same class is reused everywhere, scaled up by region (a Route 2 Hiker is weak; a Victory Road Hiker is brutal), and individuated only by a first name stamped on the class (“Hiker Russell”). Faceless villain-team members get no name at all (“Team Rocket Grunt”). A few special variants exist: **doubles** (Twins), **big-money** (Gentleman), **rematchable** (recruited/registered trainers), and **creepy obsessive “Maniac”** types (Hex Maniac, Ruin Maniac) used for flavor and theme.

**We copy all of it**, retheming the classes to a post-collapse Texas of scrappers, colonists, Raiders, Militants, and cultists, and mapping team archetypes onto our four types — **HACK, BRUTE, ARSENAL, INFO.**

## 3. BATTLING NPC CLASSES

### Early / common — the “first trainers”

**Runner.** Young topside scavengers out proving themselves. *Look:* patched jacket, oversized goggles, salvage pack. *Team:* common low-tier, BRUTE/scrap lean. *Habitat:* the Field and every early route. *Tier:* weakest. *Reward:* low credits, sometimes a spare node. *Barks:* “Found my Ohm in a dumpster — he’s still tougher than yours!” / “Aw, back to the garage…”

**Picker.** Kids who collect the small, charming Ohms — bulbs, toys, handhelds. *Look:* too-big boots, a satchel of trinkets. *Team:* tiny INFO/HACK Ohmlets. *Habitat:* town edges, fields. *Tier:* weakest. *Reward:* low. *Barks:* “Mine lights up! Wanna see?” / “He still lights up… mostly.”

**Scrapper.** The workhorse class — adult salvagers digging the ruins (our Bug-Catcher-meets-Hiker). *Look:* welding mask up, gloves, pry-bar. *Team:* tool and junk Ohms, BRUTE–HACK. *Habitat:* ruins, debris fields, collapsed interiors; everywhere. *Tier:* low-mid, common. *Reward:* salvage + credits. *Barks:* “You leave the good parts, I take ’em.” / “Strip me for scrap, then.”

### Trade & road (Railhead and the highways)

**Hauler.** Ride or drive vehicle Ohms along the trade routes; often travel in pairs (convoy guards → **double battles**). *Look:* duster, road-dust, a hand on the throttle. *Team:* vehicle/ground Ohms, BRUTE/ARSENAL. *Habitat:* roads, Railhead, parking-structure dungeons. *Tier:* mid. *Reward:* good credits. *Barks:* “Convoy’s mine to protect.” / “Road wins again.”

**Trade Baron.** Flashy market magnates (our Gentleman) — small elite teams, fat purses. *Look:* salvaged finery, too many rings. *Team:* few but strong, mixed. *Habitat:* markets, Railhead, late hubs. *Tier:* mid-high. *Reward:* **big credits.** *Barks:* “Credits are just trust, and I am very trustworthy.” / “An expensive lesson.”

### Agrarian (the Cistern, open range)

**Field-hand.** Colonists who raise Ohms gently like a herd. *Look:* sun hat, irrigation muck. *Team:* nurturing INFO/organic-leaning. *Habitat:* fields, the Cistern. *Tier:* low-mid. *Reward:* healing items. *Barks:* “Raised mine from a flicker.” / “Go on, she forgives you.”

**Wrangler.** Texan cattle-drivers who herd the big BRUTE Ohms (our Cowboy). *Look:* worn hat, lasso of cabling. *Team:* heavy BRUTE. *Habitat:* open range, Caliche outskirts. *Tier:* mid. *Reward:* credits. *Barks:* “Yeehp. Get along.” / “Spooked the whole herd, ya did.”

### Colony defense (Bastion and every colony gate)

**Sentry.** Wall-guards and gate-keepers. *Look:* scrap-plate armor, fixed stare. *Team:* durable BRUTE/ARSENAL walls. *Habitat:* colony gates, Bastion. *Tier:* mid, sturdy. *Reward:* credits. *Barks:* “None pass untested.” / “Pass, then.”

### Militarists (the villain-grunt layer — and a foreshadowing tool)

**Militant** *(faceless — “Militant A / B”)*. The Team-Rocket-Grunt analog, fought in packs. *Look:* matte-grey kit, full visor. *Team:* mixed ARSENAL/HACK. *Habitat:* patrols, Redoubt, the Dallas tower. *Tier:* low-to-high by act. *Reward:* credits. **Design hook:** their barks land a half-beat *off* and repeat oddly — “We. Serve. The order.” — seeding the puppet reveal long before the midpoint. Unnamed by design. *Barks:* “Orders are… orders.” / “…why did I… stop?”

**Enforcer.** Elite Militants with stun-tech (no guns exist). *Look:* heavier plate, telescoping baton. *Team:* tougher ARSENAL. *Habitat:* patrol leaders, mini-bosses. *Tier:* high. *Reward:* good credits. *Barks:* “Stand down.” / “Malfunction… acknowledged.”

### Cult (the Chancel, later the tower)

**Acolyte.** Converts of the Update — serene, unsettling, scripture on the lips (our creepy “Maniac” energy). *Look:* grey robes, blissful eyes. *Team:* eerie INFO/HACK, status-heavy. *Habitat:* the Chancel, the tower approach. *Tier:* mid-high. *Reward:* credits. *Barks:* “You agreed without reading.” / “Soon you will not mind.”

### Raiders & mercs (Caliche, contested ruins)

**Raider.** Aggressive scavengers who hit first (glass-cannon culture). *Look:* spiked scrap, war-paint of grease. *Team:* ARSENAL glass cannons. *Habitat:* Caliche, disputed ruins. *Tier:* mid-high, swingy. *Reward:* good credits. *Barks:* “Yours now? Mine soon.” / “Lucky hit.”

**Merc.** For-hire toughs with strong mixed teams; **rematchable** for credits. *Look:* mismatched gear, a contract pinned to the chest. *Team:* balanced, dangerous. *Habitat:* hideouts, roads. *Tier:* high. *Reward:* big credits. *Barks:* “Pay’s pay.” / “Hmf. Worth it?”

### Tech & net (the Array, uplink spots)

**Tinker.** Gadgeteers and Ohm-modders — HACK specialists. *Look:* magnifier rig, a dozen pockets. *Team:* HACK control/debuff. *Habitat:* workshops, the Array. *Tier:* mid-high. *Reward:* a patch chip sometimes. *Barks:* “Let’s see your firmware.” / “Patched, beaten, noted.”

**Signal-jockey.** Net-divers who fight from the satnet (our Psychic). *Look:* uplink visor, cables to the skull. *Team:* INFO/net Ohms, prediction and control. *Habitat:* uplink spots, the Array. *Tier:* high. *Reward:* credits. *Barks:* “I saw this fight already.” / “Static in my read…”

### Thematic / creepy (the sentience question, given a face)

**Net-Medium.** Lonely obsessives convinced the Ohms have souls — and that they can hear them (our Hex Maniac, and a quiet drumbeat for the game’s whole theme). *Look:* hollow stare, an Ohm cradled like a child. *Team:* unsettling INFO/HACK. *Habitat:* ruined chapels, the Chancel fringe, abandoned uplinks. *Tier:* mid-high. *Reward:* credits. *Barks:* “She’s been trying to tell you.” / “You heard it too. Don’t lie.”

### Veteran / elite (the back half)

**Old Hand.** Salvage veterans with deep, balanced teams (our Ace Trainer). *Look:* grey stubble, a coat of patches earned. *Team:* well-rounded, all four types represented across placements. *Habitat:* late routes, the Verge. *Tier:* highest non-boss. *Reward:* big credits. *Barks:* “Forty years topside. Show me.” / “…not bad, kid.”

**Drifter.** Lone nomads with mysterious teams that **scale to your progress**; rematchable, a recurring optional test. *Look:* faceless wrap, no colony colors. *Team:* unpredictable, mixed. *Habitat:* empty roads. *Tier:* tracks the player. *Reward:* credits. *Barks:* “…” / “We’ll meet again.”

### Pairs & in-trial

**Scrap Twins** *(pair)*. Sibling kids fought together (our Twins) — **double battles**. *Look:* matching patched overalls. *Team:* coordinated low-mid. *Habitat:* towns. *Tier:* low-mid. *Reward:* credits. *Barks:* “We share everything!” / “Even losing!”

**Warden’s Hand** *(in-trial sub-boss)*. The guards posted inside a colony trial before the Warden — the “gym trainer” layer. *Look:* that colony’s colors and culture. *Team:* leans into that colony’s type focus, a step below the Warden. *Habitat:* one to three per trial. *Tier:* scales per colony. *Reward:* credits + progress. *Barks:* themed to the colony.

## 4. SERVICE & UTILITY NPCs (non-battling)

- **Garage Tech** — the Nurse analog at every garage. Restores the party. Signature line: “You’re all recharged — stay current out there.”
- **Quartermaster** — the storefront clerk. Sells **storage nodes, Repair Kits, D-FIB, signal dampeners.**
- **Node-Monger** — specialty vendor for rarer/stronger nodes (better catch odds).
- **Salvage Buyer** — buys the player’s junk Ohm-parts and scrap for credits.
- **Name-Keeper** — the nickname station (“only at special places,” per canon).
- **Chip-Tutor** — the move-tutor analog; installs **patch chips** for credits or rare parts.
- **Net-Garage Operator** — access to the Network Garage (the box/PC system); sometimes a person at a terminal, sometimes just the terminal.
- **Scout-Master / Cartographer** — sells/gives area intel and arranges **vehicle-Ohm fast travel** between unlocked points.
- **Courier of *The Current*** — runners who deliver news, rumors, and side-quest hooks; the world’s bulletin in human form.
- **Bench-Keeper** *(post-v1 hook)* — minor part-swapping services once crafting expands beyond the starter Bench.

## 5. FLAVOR TOWNSFOLK (pure dialogue color, by colony culture)

Cheap, non-battling texture — a few archetypes per place, each just a line or two. Use them to layer dialogue and sell each colony’s philosophy without moving the plot.

- **Ohmstead (home):** worried elders, kids racing Ohmlets in the tunnels, off-duty Runners swapping finds.
- **Railhead (trade):** market barkers, dice-gamblers, road-worn Haulers nursing a drink.
- **The Cistern (cultivation):** farmhands, a cook feeding strays, nursemaids tending Ohm-younglings.
- **Bastion (isolationist):** twitchy watchmen, shut-ins who won’t open the door, a kid who’s never seen the surface.
- **Redoubt (post-reveal):** shell-shocked colonists, newly-freed soldiers blinking awake, a medic counting the cost.
- **The Chancel (the faithful):** hymn-singers, footsore pilgrims, a doubter quietly questioning the Cantor.
- **Caliche (raiders):** brawlers, scrap-gamblers, kids puffing up tough to match their elders.
- **The Array (scholars):** muttering engineers, an archivist who won’t stop talking, a net-addict lost in the uplink.
- **The Verge (memory-keepers):** ancient storytellers, refugees from fallen colonies, someone who remembers the world before.

## 7. PLACEMENT & SCALING (the reuse rules)

- **Theme by terrain & faction.** Each class belongs to its region (Scrappers in ruins, Haulers on roads, Field-hands at the Cistern, Militants on patrols, Acolytes at the Chancel, Raiders at Caliche, Tinkers/Signal-jockeys at the Array, Old Hands near the Verge). This is what makes a place *feel* like itself.
- **Scale the same class up.** A Field Runner is trivial; a late-route Runner is a real fight. Re-band team levels by progress, not by inventing new classes.
- **Spread the four types across classes** so the player meets HACK, BRUTE, ARSENAL, and INFO naturally as they travel, the way Pokémon spreads types across Bug/Water/Rock/etc.
- **Use the variants on purpose:** doubles (Hauler pairs, Scrap Twins) for tempo; **big-money** (Trade Baron, Merc) as credit faucets; **rematchable** (Drifter, Merc, Old Hand via *The Current*) for grinding; the **Net-Medium** sparingly, to keep the soul question humming under the surface.
- **The Militant bark drift is a story device** — keep it subtle and consistent so attentive players feel something is wrong with the Militarists before Colony 4 confirms it.
- **Tiering ladder (low → high):** Runner / Picker → Scrapper / Field-hand / Scrap Twins → Hauler / Sentry / Wrangler / Tinker → Raider / Acolyte / Enforcer / Signal-jockey / Net-Medium → Merc / Trade Baron / Old Hand / Drifter → **Warden’s Hand → Warden (trial boss).**
