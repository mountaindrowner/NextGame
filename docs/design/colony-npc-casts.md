# OHMFRONT — Colony NPC Casts (Local Color)

*This is the layer that gives every city its own people. The generic classes in the Variable NPCs doc fill the routes between colonies; **these** fill the colonies themselves — each with local battle classes found nowhere else, the Warden’s personal circle (their “gym trainers”), role-specific functionaries, and a couple of quirky or funny faces to make the place stick. All region-appropriate, all working names.*

**Rename locked:** the rival colony **Caliche → Redbed**; their faction/creed is **the New Order**. (Propagate to the Cast, Story Bible, and master prompt — flagged at the end.)

**How to use:** Each colony below gets four buckets — **Local Classes** (colony-flavored trainers), **The Warden’s Circle** (themed sub-bosses inside the trial), **Specific Roles** (functionaries unique to that culture), and **Quirky / Funny** (memorable oddballs). ## OHMSTEAD — home *(humble scrapper bunker under a dead cattle town)*

**Local Classes**

- **Tunnel Rat** — kids racing Ohmlets through the bunker shafts; tiny fast INFO/HACK; bet bottle caps.
- **Greenhorn** — first-time Raiders barely older than you; weak BRUTE/scrap, all nerves.

**The Warden’s Circle — Boone’s Deputies (the Cattlemen’s Watch)**

- Boone’s old guard, posted at the elevator and the gate; sturdy BRUTE walls; the tutorial’s friendly resistance.

**Specific Roles**

- **Elevator Operator** — controls the lift to the surface; the gatekeeper to your first Ohm Run.
- **Bunker Quartermaster** — first shop; sells your starter nodes and Repair Kits.
- **Bench-Keeper** — tends Grandpa’s workshop alongside Grandma Mabel.

**Quirky / Funny**

- **“Old Tuck”** — half-deaf retiree insisting his rusted toaster-Ohm “still’s got it” (it does not); recurring comic check-ins.
- **The Wall-Painter** — a kid muraling Ohm history on the tunnel walls and narrating every fact slightly wrong.

## RAILHEAD — Colony 1 *(crossroads market on an old rail junction)*

**Local Classes**

- **Rail-runner** — kids who sprint the tracks; speedy INFO Ohms.
- **Tollman** — “taxes” passage; battle if you won’t pay; durable ARSENAL, credit-obsessed.
- **Card Sharp** — bets on battles (the Gambler analog); swingy teams, double-or-nothing payouts.

**The Warden’s Circle — the Brokers (Dell Marrow’s trade-guild)**

- Each Broker corners one commodity and fights to match: **the Salt Broker** (attrition/stall), **the Scrap Broker** (BRUTE bruisers), **the Water Broker** (defensive control). Balanced, shrewd, a real first-trial wall.

**Specific Roles**

- **Auctioneer** — runs the market in a fast-talking blur.
- **Switchman** — throws the rail switches that open new routes (gate/puzzle NPC).
- **Freight Master** — dispatches vehicle-Ohm fast travel once you’ve earned it.

**Quirky / Funny**

- **“Two-Bit” Birdie** — tiny old woman who’ll wager on anything, loses every time, never stops grinning.
- **The Mime-Courier** — lost his voice to the Static; delivers every message in elaborate, increasingly desperate charades.

## THE CISTERN — Colony 2 *(gentle agrarian colony in flooded underground waterworks)*

**Local Classes**

- **Sower** — “plants” and tends Ohms like crops; nurturing INFO/organic.
- **Cistern-keeper** — water-tenders with slippery, defensive Ohms (our closest thing to Water-flavor).
- **Drover** — moves Ohm-herds between chambers; steady BRUTE.

**The Warden’s Circle — the Grange (Etta Bloom’s council-hands)**

- Healing-heavy attrition teams; each tends a different “crop” of Ohm and fights to outlast you. Teaches patience as a mechanic.

**Specific Roles**

- **Hydromancer** — runs the water system that keeps the colony alive.
- **Nursery-Matron** — raises Ohmlets; hands the player a gift Ohm (the in-region “egg” moment).
- **Seed-Keeper** — archivist of Ohm “bloodlines” and where each line is found.

**Quirky / Funny**

- **“Mud” Cole** — farmhand caked head-to-toe, treats his Ohms like spoiled toddlers.
- **The Weather-Watcher** — old man who predicts the EM shimmer rolling in and is reliably, confidently wrong.

## BASTION — Colony 3 *(paranoid quarry-fortress that trusts no one)*

**Local Classes**

- **Wall-watch** — twitchy sentries; defensive BRUTE/ARSENAL walls.
- **Quarryman** — stone-cutters swinging heavy BRUTE Ohms (the Hiker of this world).
- **Gate-keeper** — several posted in sequence; none let you pass without a fight (a gating gauntlet).

**The Warden’s Circle — the Stoneguard (Calder Stone’s elite)**

- Immovable defensive teams — the hardest “wall” fights of the early-mid game. Beating them is how you earn Bastion’s grudging respect.

**Specific Roles**

- **Warden of the Gate** — bureaucratically controls entry; demands the right papers/proof.
- **Rationer** — hoards supplies behind a slot; suspicious, overpriced vendor.
- **Lookout** — atop the quarry rim; trades intel for favors.

**Quirky / Funny**

- **“Knock-Twice” Nell** — shut-in who speaks only through a door-slot and demands an ever-changing secret knock.
- **The Doomsayer** — ranting prophet of the end who’s been wrong for thirty years and will not be told.

## REDOUBT — Colony 4 *(the colony that trusted the Militarists — the midpoint)*

**Local Classes**

- **Conscript** — colonists pressed into Militarist service; glassy and off-tempo before the reveal, freed and apologetic after.
- **Base-scrapper** — scavenging the old military installation; ARSENAL salvage.
- **Deserter** — soldiers who broke the Static early and went to ground; strong, jittery ARSENAL.

**The Warden’s Circle — the Garrison Remnant (Pike & Reyes, post-reveal)**

- Freed soldiers rallying around Commander Reyes; disciplined mixed teams, a few still shaking the hive from their heads. The trial here doubles as the reveal’s emotional aftermath.

**Specific Roles**

- **Field-Medic** — patches the wounded and counts the cost; healing vendor.
- **Signal-Officer** — intercepts PERSISTENCE’s broadcasts; a window into the enemy.
- **Bunker-Archivist** — keeper of the logs that expose the truth (plot-adjacent).

**Quirky / Funny**

- **“Sarge”** — a freed soldier who can’t recall his own name after the Static and cheerfully picks a new one every morning (bittersweet).
- **The Re-Enlister** — so conditioned to orders he begs passersby to give him chores just to feel useful.

## THE CHANCEL — Colony 5 *(machine-worship cult in a ruined megachurch — and a schism)*

**Local Classes**

- **Chorister** — robed singers who “sing” to their Ohms; eerie INFO/status teams.
- **Deacon** — the true Chancel’s defenders (Verity Hale’s side); protective INFO.
- **Convert** — the Cantor’s splinter; self-sacrificing HACK, unsettling and serene.

**The Warden’s Circle — the Vestry (Verity Hale’s inner circle)**

- Wise, support-heavy, defensive teams; each embodies a “virtue.” The gentlest-feeling trial with surprising teeth.

**Antagonist Sub-Group — the Choir of the Update (the Cantor’s)**

- Converts who battle to “deliver” you to the hive; their welcome grows colder and stranger the deeper you go.

**Specific Roles**

- **Bell-Keeper** — rings the old church bells as the colony’s signal language.
- **Reliquary-Warden** — guards the Chancel’s revered ancient “saint” Ohm.
- **Confessor** — hears doubts; the human cost of the schism, in dialogue.

**Quirky / Funny**

- **“Brother Hum”** — a monk who communicates only in tuneful humming that everyone somehow understands perfectly.
- **The Apostate** — an ex-convert who loudly heckles the Cantor’s sermons and feeds the player sarcastic truth.

## REDBED — Colony 6 *(rival raider colony on the red hardpan — the New Order — Rook’s home)*

*Faction note: the **New Order** is Redbed’s creed — strength, self-rule, no masters, no mercy for the weak. New Order raiders also cross your path **outside** Redbed, on contested routes, as recurring antagonist-flavored fights (like Rook himself). After the hive takes Redbed, these same people reappear displaced and broken, and the funny ones land sadder.*

**Local Classes**

- **Redbed Raider** — the core aggressor; ARSENAL glass cannons under red war-dust.
- **Dust-runner** — hit-and-run kids on scrap-bikes; fast ARSENAL/vehicle.
- **Claim-jumper** — steals others’ finds; battle gimmick built around swiping items/credits on contact.
- **Bone-picker** — works the deep dangerous ruins; tough mixed teams.

**The Warden’s Circle — the Red Hand (Warden Sol’s champions / New Order elite)**

- Ferocious all-offense teams; Rook’s peers and rivals. Three named toughs to anchor it: **Ladder** (climbs over anyone), **Scorch** (all-in OVERHEAT pressure), **Pell** (see below).

**Specific Roles**

- **Scrap-Boss** — runs Redbed’s brutal salvage economy; barters like a knife fight.
- **Bike-Wright** — builds the scrap-bikes and vehicle Ohms the runners ride.
- **Pit-Caller** — runs the battle-pit where the New Order settles every dispute.

**Quirky / Funny**

- **“Big Talk” Pell** — scrawniest raider, biggest mouth, weakest team; pure bravado, secretly a coward.
- **The Sentimental Raider** — a terrifying slab of a man who quietly knits little cozies for his Ohms.

## THE ARRAY — Colony 7 *(scholar-engineers in an old data center who understand the towers)*

**Local Classes**

- **Net-diver** — home turf for the class; INFO/net specialists.
- **Coder** — software-tinkers; HACK control and debuff.
- **Antenna-tech** — climb the old towers to fight; ARSENAL/signal.
- **Data-ghost** — net-obsessives who barely leave the uplink; strange, drifting INFO.

**The Warden’s Circle — the Lattice (Tobias Frost’s senior engineers)**

- Clever, control-heavy teams that abuse status, weather, and field tricks — the most genuinely tactical fights before the finale.

**Specific Roles**

- **Uplink-Operator** — runs net access; the gateway to net encounters.
- **Cartographer** — keeps the old-world maps; reveals the region and PERSISTENCE’s tower network.
- **Power-Keeper** — nurses the data center’s failing reactor; the colony’s fragile lifeline.

**Quirky / Funny**

- **“Buffering” Bex** — a net-diver who talks in laggy, glitching half-sentences from too long jacked in.
- **The Conspiracy-Coder** — certain the satellites are watching everyone (he’s half right), dispensing ominous half-truths.

## THE VERGE — Colony 8 *(the oldest colony, memory-keepers, under siege at Dallas’s edge)*

**Local Classes**

- **Old-blood** — veteran defenders with deep balanced teams (Verge-flavored Old Hands).
- **Refugee-Raider** — displaced fighters from fallen colonies, Redbed survivors among them; desperate, strong, mixed.
- **Vergewatch** — siege defenders manning the last wall; durable ARSENAL.

**The Warden’s Circle — the Keepers (Ruth Amos’s eldest guardians)**

- Veteran teams that play like a final exam before Dallas; each Keeper carries a fragment of pre-collapse memory dropped in their pre-battle line.

**Specific Roles**

- **Lorekeeper** — oral historian; the deepest world lore and the last Grandpa hints.
- **Beacon-Warden** — tends the signal beacon that calls the colonies to gather.
- **Convoy-Master** — organizes the march on Dallas; staging and fast-travel hub.

**Quirky / Funny**

- **“Old Mesa”** — claims to be 130 and to have personally known everyone famous; tall tales that occasionally hide a real clue.
- **The Reunion-Keeper** — obsessively perfecting Ohmcoming festival details mid-siege (“the bunting must hang *straight*, the world is ending, not slovenly”).
