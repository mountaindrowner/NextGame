# OHMFRONT — Critical Path: Regions, Routes & Locations

*The required journey from point A to point Z, fitted to the plot — every town, colony, road, cave, dungeon, and building of note the player must pass to beat the game, in order. The wider optional world comes later; this is the spine.*

**Region name (working):** **the Blackland** — the ruined Blackland Prairie south of Dallas, arcing north into the dead metroplex. *(Distinct from the colony-network name, still open. Dallas is the region's dark pole.)*

**Geography:** The journey runs roughly south-to-north. It opens in cattle-and-grain country, climbs through prairie and quarry land, crosses the drowned Trinity bottoms, threads the ruined sprawl, and ends in the downtown canyon at the tower. Each colony sits where its real-world kind would: traders on a rail junction, quarry-folk in the cement lands, scholars in the northern data corridor, the last holdout at the city's edge.

**Gating philosophy (per canon):** Progress is gated mostly by **story** (clear a colony's trial → the road onward opens) and a few **key items**. **Exactly one field ability hard-gates the path — HOVER** (crossing water on a vehicle Ohm). The others (SHEAR, BREACH, HAUL, LUMEN) exist for **optional** shortcuts, side-caches, and rare-Ohm nooks, never the main road. **Vehicle Ohms** are the big traversal unlock and power fast travel once cleared.

**Per-stop format:** *Type · Terrain & encounters · Places of note · Gate in · Plot beat · Leads to.*

# ACT I — THE MESSENGER

### 0. OHMSTEAD — home colony *(underground bunker beneath a dead cattle town)*
- **Type:** Hub (no trial — home).
- **Terrain:** Tunnels and machine-shops below; the surface above is the prologue zone.
- **Places of note:** **the Bench** (Grandpa's workshop, where you build your first Ohm); **the Elevator** (the only lift up); **the Cattlemen's Watch** gate; the bunker market.
- **Gate in:** Start.
- **Plot beat:** Coming of age → build and name your first Ohm → the supply-run tutorial → the night signal → the scout fight → **the Breaker bonds** → exile.
- **Leads to:** The Field.

### 1. THE FIELD — prologue overworld *(ruined cattle town on open blackland prairie)*
- **Type:** Tutorial route + first dungeon.
- **Terrain:** Tall prairie grass, debris piles, EM shimmer zones in the dead houses; invisible random encounters of low-tier prairie Ohms.
- **Places of note:** the cattle town's dead main street; **the Co-op Vault** (the safe that holds the Breaker, broken open in the prologue fight); **Ohm Depot** (the orange hardware-warehouse mega-dungeon full of tool Ohms — optional but iconic, introduced here).
- **Gate in:** Story (the elevator opens).
- **Plot beat:** Learn to catch and battle; the **Ohmwork** index assignment from Odessa; first real wild captures.
- **Leads to:** Farm Road north.

### 2. THE FARM ROAD — route *(cracked farm-to-market blacktop)*
- **Type:** Route.
- **Terrain:** Prairie and fence-line grass; Runner and Scrapper encounters.
- **Places of note:** an abandoned **cotton gin**; a climbable **water tower** (lookout); the start of the old rail line.
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** First wild human battles; the world opens up.
- **Leads to:** Railhead.

### 3. RAILHEAD — Colony 1 *(crossroads trade-town on an old rail junction)*
- **Type:** Colony + Trial 1.
- **Terrain:** Rail yard and market sprawl.
- **Places of note:** **the Rail Yard / Roundhouse** (the trial dungeon — tool and scrap Ohms, the Brokers, Warden Dell Marrow); the market; the switch-house.
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** Win the colony's trust by clearing a **sabotaged relay** (Captain Holt, first Militant officer — visibly "off"). **Token 1.** Marrow's warning: the wild Ohms are coordinating.
- **Leads to:** The 45 Scar.

### 4. THE 45 SCAR — route *(a ruined interstate, miles of dead cars)*
- **Type:** Route + traversal unlock.
- **Terrain:** Wrecked highway; Hauler convoys, vehicle Ohms.
- **Places of note:** **the Hertz Lot** (rental-car ruin — prime vehicle-Ohm hunting); **Mobile Ohm Park** (trailer-park nest where vehicle Ohms gather). **This is where you catch your first vehicle Ohm.**
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** The Pull begins — wild Ohms start drifting toward you when you linger.
- **Leads to:** The Cistern.

### 5. THE CISTERN — Colony 2 *(gentle agrarian colony in flooded underground waterworks)*
- **Type:** Colony + Trial 2 · **THE field-ability gate (HOVER).**
- **Terrain:** Flooded chambers and channels; you need a **vehicle Ohm with HOVER** to cross the water — the one true field-ability gate of the game.
- **Places of note:** **the Flooded Cisterns** (the trial dungeon — defensive/fluid Ohms, the Grange, Warden Etta Bloom); the Nursery; the Seed-vault.
- **Gate in:** Story → then HOVER to reach the trial's heart.
- **Plot beat:** Clue that the Militarists are fractured, some acting against their own will. **Token 2.** The Nursery-Matron gifts you an Ohm.
- **Leads to:** The Furrows.

### 6. THE FURROWS — route *(open blackland prairie, old cotton country)*
- **Type:** Route.
- **Terrain:** Tall grass, furrowed fields; Field-hand and Wrangler encounters.
- **Places of note:** a crashed **crop-duster**; long fence-lines; a lone silo.
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** Breather; the prairie gives way to stone country.
- **Leads to:** Bastion.

### 7. BASTION — Colony 3 *(paranoid quarry-fortress in the cement lands)*
- **Type:** Colony + Trial 3.
- **Terrain:** Quarry terraces, concrete works.
- **Places of note:** **the Pit** (the quarry trial dungeon — heavy BRUTE Ohms, the Stoneguard, Warden Calder Stone; optional BREACH side-rooms hide rare Ohms); the great Wall; the cement plant.
- **Gate in:** Story (prove yourself at the gate).
- **Plot beat:** First real taste of **deep-hive feral Ohms** besieging the walls — Ohms the normal hack can't reach, quietly proving why the Breaker will matter. **Token 3.**
- **Leads to:** The Military Road.

### 8. THE MILITARY ROAD — route *(an old supply road thick with Militant patrols)*
- **Type:** Route.
- **Terrain:** Checkpoint ruins; first heavy Militant presence; Conscript and Enforcer encounters.
- **Places of note:** a ruined **checkpoint**; burned patrol vehicles.
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** The Militarist threat feels at its peak — right before it inverts.
- **Leads to:** Redoubt.

### 9. REDOUBT & THE BUNKER — Colony 4 *(the colony that trusted the Militarists — THE MIDPOINT)*
- **Type:** Colony + Trial 4 + **the reveal dungeon.**
- **Terrain:** The colony above; **the Bunker** below — a deep decommissioned military base.
- **Places of note:** **the Bunker / Site B** (the dungeon: blast doors, dark halls best lit with optional LUMEN, Militant patrols, and the **command center** where you find Commander Reyes, the audio logs, PERSISTENCE's first direct words, and the first trace of **Eli Vane**). This is plausibly the very kind of bunker where the Militarists first dug PERSISTENCE up.
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** **THE REVEAL** — the Militarists are puppets; PERSISTENCE is real; Reyes is freed and joins; a photograph and a logbook name hint at Grandpa. **Token 4.**
- **Leads to:** The Trinity Bottoms.

# ACT II — THE HUNTED

### 10. THE TRINITY BOTTOMS — route/dungeon *(a drowned bottomland forest)*
- **Type:** Route-dungeon · **HOVER reused.**
- **Terrain:** Flooded forest, overgrowth (optional SHEAR shortcuts), eerie and beautiful; new **organic-hybrid** Ohms appear only here.
- **Places of note:** the **Drowned Forest** (where the **ORGANIC-HYBRID legendary** — a virus-touched tree — can be found, off the main line); sunken roads; a half-submerged chapel.
- **Gate in:** Story → HOVER across the flooded stretches.
- **Plot beat:** The world turns strange and grieving; the journey's tone deepens after the reveal.
- **Leads to:** The Chancel.

### 11. THE CHANCEL — Colony 5 *(machine-worship cult in a ruined megachurch — and a schism)*
- **Type:** Colony + Trial 5.
- **Terrain:** The cathedral ruin: nave, bell tower, catacombs.
- **Places of note:** **the Nave** (the trial — the Vestry, Warden Verity Hale); **the Catacombs** (the schism dungeon — the Cantor's Choir of the Update); **the Reliquary** (the colony's revered ancient "saint" Ohm).
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** The theology of the reveal — these people knew all along; **the Cantor** is introduced as the willing human face of the enemy; stop his rite. **Token 5.**
- **Leads to:** The Red Flats.

### 12. THE RED FLATS — route *(dry red-earth badlands)*
- **Type:** Route.
- **Terrain:** Cracked red hardpan, dust; **New Order raiders** ambush here.
- **Places of note:** scrap-bike wrecks; a raider toll-arch.
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** Redbed's raiders harry you — the rival colony's hostility at its sharpest.
- **Leads to:** Redbed.

### 13. REDBED — Colony 6 *(rival raider colony on the hardpan — the New Order — Rook's home)*
- **Type:** Colony + Trial 6 *(siege)*.
- **Terrain:** A raider stronghold half-overrun by the hive when you arrive.
- **Places of note:** **the Battle-Pit** (where the New Order settles disputes — and where the trial plays out); the Bike-works; the Red Hand's roost (Warden Sol). The trial doubles as **fighting through a hive assault** alongside Sol and Rook.
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** Redbed falls to the Static; **Rook's home is taken**; rival becomes ally; the cost of the hive lands on a familiar face. **Token 6.**
- **Leads to:** The Tollway Ruins.

### 14. THE TOLLWAY RUINS — route *(ruined toll roads on the city's edge)*
- **Type:** Route.
- **Terrain:** The first true urban sprawl — overpasses, on-ramps, dead toll plazas; Merc and Drifter encounters.
- **Places of note:** stacked **overpass ramps**; a toppled toll gantry.
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** The ruins of the old metroplex begin; the air hums louder.
- **Leads to:** The Array.

### 15. THE ARRAY — Colony 7 *(scholar-engineers in a northern data-center campus)*
- **Type:** Colony + Trial 7.
- **Terrain:** Server halls, cooling towers, an antenna farm.
- **Places of note:** **the Server Halls** (the trial — the Lattice, Warden Tobias Frost, the most tactical fight pre-finale; a **PROTOTYPE** legendary can lurk in the experimental wing); **the Uplink Chamber** (net encounters); the antenna farm where the tower plan is laid bare.
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** The **full threat** is explained — towers → the Dallas antenna → the satnet upload; **Mag works out the Breaker can broadcast through the tower.** The endgame plan is born. **Token 7.**
- **Leads to:** The Stack.

### 16. THE STACK — route-dungeon *(a colossal ruined highway interchange)*
- **Type:** Vertical route-dungeon · *tunnel/overpass battles.*
- **Terrain:** A five-level tangle of collapsed stacked overpasses — a climbing maze of road-battles; heavy hive presence; vehicle Ohms shine.
- **Places of note:** the stacked decks; a jackknifed convoy; service tunnels beneath (the user's "tunnel battles").
- **Gate in:** Story (the convoy forms here).
- **Plot beat:** The united convoy pushes toward the city; the Pull is now a visible storm of wild Ohms trailing the column.
- **Leads to:** The Verge.

### 17. THE VERGE — Colony 8 *(the oldest colony, fortified at Dallas's edge, under siege)*
- **Type:** Colony + Trial 8.
- **Terrain:** Old walls around a fortified rail terminal at the city's southern lip.
- **Places of note:** **the Walls** and **the Beacon** (that calls the colonies to gather); the **Convoy Staging Ground**; the Lorekeeper's archive (Warden Ruth Amos; an **ANCIENT** legendary may be kept/guarded here).
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** **Grandpa confirmed** — Eli uploaded himself into the net and may be holding the line still; the convoy assembles for the final push. **Token 8.**
- **Leads to:** Dallas — the Outer Ring.

# ACT III — TOWARD THE TOWER (DALLAS)

### 18. THE OUTER RING — dungeon-zone *(Dallas's hive-saturated outskirts)*
- **Type:** Hostile wild zone.
- **Terrain:** Ruined neighborhoods drowning in Static; **feral, near-unhackable Ohms** everywhere — the most dangerous wilds in the game; the skyline of dead towers ahead.
- **Places of note:** burned suburbs; the first fallen skyscrapers; hive relay-towers humming.
- **Gate in:** Story (the convoy breaches the ring).
- **Plot beat:** Fight through the worst of the Static toward downtown.
- **Leads to:** The Canyon.

### 19. THE CANYON — dungeon *(downtown Dallas, the skyscraper ruins)*
- **Type:** Urban dungeon — **a skyscraper climb.**
- **Terrain:** The dead financial district; a chosen **collapsed skyscraper** the player ascends (and partly descends through its broken floors) to cross the impassable street-level wreckage.
- **Places of note:** the **leaning tower** (the skyscraper dungeon — vertical, hazardous, hive champions on the upper floors); an open **plaza** below; the dead West End.
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** The final approach; the tower comes into view across the canyon.
- **Leads to:** The Underground.

### 20. THE UNDERGROUND — dungeon *(the downtown pedestrian tunnel network)*
- **Type:** Tunnel dungeon — **tunnel battles.**
- **Terrain:** The sealed underground pedestrian tunnels linking the towers; claustrophobic; the last stand of the **hive-loyal Militants** under **Marshal Drake**.
- **Places of note:** the tunnel junctions; a flooded concourse; the blast-sealed door to the tower's base.
- **Gate in:** Story.
- **Plot beat:** Drake's remnant defends the tower from below; the convoy fights through to the spire's foundation.
- **Leads to:** The Spire.

### 21. THE SPIRE — final dungeon *(Reunion Tower, retrofitted as PERSISTENCE's antenna)*
- **Type:** Final dungeon + final boss.
- **Terrain:** The iconic ball-on-a-stalk tower, old steel fused with hive-grown flesh; an ascent through a **gauntlet of Ohmega-class hive champions** to the antenna chamber at the crown.
- **Places of note:** the **stalk** (the climb); **the Crown** (the antenna chamber — the final arena).
- **Gate in:** Story → the **Breaker** is the only thing that can cut the tower's deep Static (the Breaker hard-gates the very end).
- **Plot beat:** The two-front finale — **the Monad** (Prototype-class avatar) descends as Drake's remnant attacks; **Reyes's holding sacrifice** buys the way up. Final battle vs. the Monad = the node-puzzle hack at maximum scale; **Grandpa's voice breaks through the Static**; the Breaker uploads through the tower into the satnet. **The Static clears worldwide.**
- **Leads to:** Resolution.

### 22. OHMCOMING — closing *(back home at Ohmstead)*
- **Type:** Epilogue location.
- **Terrain:** The surface above Ohmstead, alive for the first time.
- **Places of note:** the festival grounds; Grandpa's bench; the gathered colonies.
- **Plot beat:** The first **Ohmcoming** — celebration, mourning, a shared surface; Grandpa's last *Ohm's Law* quote. Hooks: PERSISTENCE still whispers from the mountains; Grandpa, still in the net — *"now free me."*

Ohmstead → the Field → Farm Road → **Railhead (T1)** → the 45 Scar *(first vehicle Ohm)* → **the Cistern (T2 · HOVER gate)** → the Furrows → **Bastion (T3)** → Military Road → **Redoubt + the Bunker (T4 · REVEAL)** → the Trinity Bottoms → **the Chancel (T5)** → the Red Flats → **Redbed (T6 · siege)** → Tollway Ruins → **the Array (T7 · the plan)** → the Stack → **the Verge (T8 · Grandpa confirmed)** → the Outer Ring → the Canyon *(skyscraper)* → the Underground *(tunnels)* → **the Spire / Reunion Tower (FINALE)** → Ohmcoming.
