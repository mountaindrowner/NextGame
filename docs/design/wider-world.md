# OHMFRONT — The Wider World & Optional Content

*Everything off the critical path, fully fleshed the way a Pokémon game fills the space around its main road: optional side-areas, side-quest systems, facilities and minigame zones, optional legendaries, the whole post-game, and the bigger/farther world the ending only hints at. All fitted to established lore; all optional unless noted.*

**Scope flag:** Sections 1–6 are **in-game-1 optional content** (build for the full release). Section 7 is **hinted-only** — sequel/DLC seeds planted in dialogue and lore, not built in v1.

---

## 1. OPTIONAL SIDE-AREAS (branching off the Blackland critical path)

These hang off the routes and colonies and exist to reward exploration. This is where the **optional** field abilities finally earn their keep — SHEAR (cut), BREACH (rock), HAUL (haul debris), LUMEN (light) — none of them gate the main road.

- **Ohm Depot** *(off the Field)* — the orange hardware-warehouse mega-dungeon: multi-floor aisles of tool Ohms, LUMEN for the dark back-stock, a Crane-Ohm mini-boss, strong early gear.
- **The Stockyards** *(Ohmstead surface)* — cattle pens and a slaughterhouse ruin; HAUL the fallen gates aside; a den of heavy BRUTE herd-Ohms and a tough optional brawl.
- **The Drive-In** *(off Railhead)* — a ruined drive-in theater; LUMEN powers the old projector and draws console- and screen-Ohms out for a night-only event; a rare INFO Ohm.
- **The Aquifer Deeps** *(below the Cistern)* — deeper flooded caverns past HOVER water and LUMEN dark; rare net/water Ohms and a quiet legendary spring.
- **The Vein** *(below Bastion's Pit)* — BREACH-gated deep quarry; fossil-like relic Ohms and an **ANCIENT-class** lair; a light mining-route puzzle.
- **Site C — the Silo** *(off Redoubt)* — a second buried military site; HAUL and LUMEN; dangerous, and the richest **origin lore** on PERSISTENCE and the old weapons program; a PROTOTYPE-tier reward.
- **The Bog Shrine** *(in the Trinity Bottoms)* — a SHEAR-gated drowned grove; the **ORGANIC-HYBRID legendary's** deeper lair; eerie and beautiful.
- **The Reliquary Deeps** *(below the Chancel)* — extended catacombs holding the cult's buried history and the "saint" Ohm side-quest.
- **The Boneyard** *(off Redbed)* — an aircraft-and-vehicle graveyard; BREACH in; vehicle-Ohm rares, raider spawns, and the first tease of a **flight-capable Ohm** (FLIGHT comes later).
- **Cold Storage** *(below the Array)* — a deep server vault; **PROTOTYPE** Ohms, the uplink-diving hub, and sealed pre-collapse data that seeds the origin mystery.
- **The Stadium** *(urban, near the Verge)* — a ruined football stadium turned optional battle arena, packed with spectator NPCs; a mid-tier challenge run.

## 2. FACILITIES & MINIGAME ZONES

The Safari / Game-Corner / Battle-Tower layer, retooled for this world.

- **The Open Range** *(Safari Zone analog)* — a fenced wildland preserve with its own rules: limited nodes, no KO-ing, **scanning and baiting** to coax rare, skittish Ohms into range. Run by a keeper who tracks the preserve's bloodlines.
- **The Pit** *(Game-Corner analog — skill-based, not slots)* — a battle-wager hall (neutral at Railhead early; Redbed's reopened battle-pit after the alliance). Win **skill battles** for tokens, trade tokens for rare Ohms, patch chips, and Bench parts. Deliberately *not* a slot machine — wagering is on battle outcomes, age-appropriate.
- **The Gauntlet** *(Battle-Tower analog — post-game)* — an endless ranked battle facility run by a neutral broker; streaks earn ranks and exclusive rewards. Difficulty scales past the main game.
- **Uplink Diving** *(recurring activity)* — the net-encounter system fleshed into a real pastime: specific uplinks across the map host **net-only Ohms**, data-treasure, and persistent "ghost in the net" rumors — a deniable early thread of Grandpa, payable off post-game.
- **Field Day** *(Contest/Showcase analog — optional, light)* — colonies host non-combat Ohm trials judged on strength, signal clarity, and speed; cosmetic titles and small rewards. A gentle change of pace.

## 3. SIDE-QUEST SYSTEMS (and named highlights)

**Recurring systems:**
- **Ohmsick visits** *(canon)* — return freed Ohms to their original sites; a repeatable chain across the whole region.
- **The Current's Errands** — couriers hand out delivery, rumor, and fetch quests that stitch the colonies together.
- **Lost Ohmlet** — track a child's runaway Ohmlet; one in nearly every town (the "lost item" staple).
- **The Collector's Registry** — scan target Ohms for a traveling archivist; Ohmdex-completion rewards.
- **Faction Favor** — help the scrapper guild, the Chancel, or a colony with their troubles for standing and gear.
- **Parts Unknown** *(Build-A-MAC)* — hunt rare locomotion and chassis parts in the optional areas to unlock new starter-build options at the Bench.
- **NPC Trades** — swap Ohms with locals for region-locked species (straight species trades — **no** trade-evolution triggers, per canon).

**Named highlights:**
- **The Scrapper's Legacy** *(the Grandpa thread — multi-part, optional, high reward)* — in every colony, someone who knew **Eli Vane** gives a fragment of his story and a keepsake. Finishing it *before* the reveal deepens the gut-punch, and rewards a one-of-a-kind Ohm built from Grandpa's signature parts.
- **The Sleepless Uplink** *(mystery)* — a haunted uplink leaving fragmentary messages; the earliest deniable Grandpa hint for sharp-eyed players.
- **The Diner's Heart** *(Ohmsick)* — bring a freed microwave-Ohm back to its dead diner, where it "cooks" one last meal for the ghost of its old routine.
- **Ring the Bell** *(Ohmsick)* — restore a schoolhouse bell-Ohm so the colony kids get a "first day" again.
- **Code Blue** *(Ohmsick)* — a hospital defibrillator-Ohm wants to go home; quietly explains where **D-FIB** revives come from.
- **The Forgotten Soldier** *(Redoubt)* — help a freed Militant piece back together who he was before the Static took him.
- **The Apostate's Proof** *(the Chancel)* — help the heckler expose the Cantor's manipulation for a faction-favor reward.
- **Bike Wars** *(Redbed)* — clear the New Order's battle-pit circuit to win the Red Hand's respect and a vehicle Ohm.

## 4. OPTIONAL LEGENDARIES & RARE ENCOUNTERS

The three legendary classes, expanded into findable encounters (a trio structure per class, Pokémon-style):

- **ANCIENT** — relics of the deep machine age: one in **the Vein**, one sealed in **Site C**, and one guarded by the Keepers at **the Verge**.
- **PROTOTYPE** — never-released experiments: one in the Array's experimental wing, one in **Cold Storage**, and **the Monad** itself — catchable only **post-game** once severed.
- **ORGANIC-HYBRID** — the virus crossed into living things: the **Bog Shrine** tree, plus hidden groves in the Trinity Bottoms and a Stadium-overgrowth specimen.
- **Roaming rares** — two or three legendary-tier Ohms wander the Blackland on the move, requiring tracking via the map and *The Current*.

## 5. POST-GAME (after the Static clears)

- **The Mountain Fortress** *(the ultimate dungeon)* — PERSISTENCE's remote stronghold, finally reachable. The hardest gauntlet in the game, deepest origin lore, and a final confrontation with the severed, isolated PERSISTENCE — one voice where there were millions.
- **The "Free Me" Quest** *(the true-ending thread)* — the post-game heart: a way to pull **Grandpa out of the net**, running through uplink dives → the Array → the satnet → the fortress's link to the sky. The emotional climax the main game sets up, and the game's true ending.
- **The Wardens' Circuit** — all 8 Wardens and key allies become rematchable at high level; a champion-tier run.
- **The Gauntlet** — the endless battle facility opens at full scaling.
- **Legendary Hunts** — the roaming and static legendaries above unlock for real pursuit.
- **Redbed Reborn** — the New Order's survivors choose a new path; a faction epilogue with consequences for the region.
- **Protocols Stirring** — the first faint signs of the "older protocols" begin as a rumor chain, the bridge to the wider world.

## 6. WORLD TEXTURE (the small stuff that sells a lived-in region)

- **Hidden items** everywhere with a subtle shimmer; itemfinder rewards.
- **Time/weather flavor** — some optional Ohms and events appear only at night, only in EM-shimmer weather, etc.
- **Readable world** — colony bulletin boards, *Ohm's Law* margin notes left in dungeons, Militant graffiti that drifts from menacing to confused (the puppet thread), cult scripture on the Chancel walls.
- **Trainer rematches** via *The Current* registry — register memorable generic trainers to fight again as they level up.

## 7. THE WIDER WORLD — HINTED, FARTHER *(sequel/DLC seeds, not built in v1)*

The ending is explicit: PERSISTENCE was one node, there are protocols older than it, other regions still hum, and the virus's origin is unknown. Plant all of it; build none of it yet.

**Distant regions** *(picked up on The Current, carried by traders and refugees):*
- **The Drowned Coast** — a flooded Gulf megacity of aquatic and oil-rig Ohms under endless storms.
- **The Turbine Plains** — the wind-farm Panhandle, vast and empty, home to turbine- and dust-Ohms and a nomad culture.
- **The Cold Grid** — a colder, more intact tech region to the north, where the AIs (and maybe the virus) may have first been made.
- **The Sister Mountains** — a western range where **other weapons-AI protocols sleep** — PERSISTENCE's siblings, the sequel antagonists.

**The deeper mysteries:**
- **The Origin** — where the nanotech actually came from: hints of an orbital or foreign source, the satellites' true purpose, a pre-collapse research program (seeded in Cold Storage, Site C, and the fortress).
- **The Other Protocols** — the ancient sibling-AIs; "protocols older than me."
- **The Sky** — the satnet's deeper layers and what else orbits up there — Grandpa's domain, and perhaps the linchpin of the whole world's fate.
