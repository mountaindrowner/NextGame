Reading the chart

Mix high and lowThe overhead and low-jab starters do almost the same damage, so alternate them on someone who is blocking.
Extra jabsEach extra jab adds only about 1 damage, because damage scales down later in a combo. The 4-hit chain mostly builds meter and hit count.
Spending meterA third of the bar on EX Rush adds about 3 damage. The full bar on a super cancel adds about 13 to 16. A level-2 Focus crumple is the cheapest way to start a big combo.
Rush on cooldown?Kick, then A (Légua Finish) knocks down without using Rush's cooldown. It does less damage (18 vs 23), so use it while Rush recharges, or EX Rush, which ignores the cooldown.
