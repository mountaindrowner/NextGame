# OHMFRONT — Full Beat Sheet (Phase 2)

*CANON — approved 2026-06-12; replaces GDD §5. Hours are targets for
the ~30-hour single run. Gates marked ⟦…⟧.*

-----

## ACT I — THE FIELD (hrs 1–8)

1. **Grounded (hr 0–1).** Ohmstead, underground. The protagonist's topside
   stunt (cold open, played for laughs) ends in a grounding. Grandpa Harlan
   covers, takes them to the garage. *Ohm's Law* Act I quote. **The Bench:**
   pick Locomotion, Core, Plating — the starter wakes and chooses a chirp for
   the player. Tutorial: movement, menus, party of one.
2. **The Elevator (hr 1).** Sneak past the freight curfew up to **the Field**.
   First daylight. Tutorial battles vs. scavenger commons; Banjo plays the
   two-note hello as send-off.
3. **First Capture (hr 1–2).** A wild Toastlet. Teach the loop: weaken → spend
   node → timed path-puzzle → **clear the static**. It shakes off the hum and
   joins. Archivist Vera assigns the **Ohmwork**: fill **the Manifest**.
4. **Working the Field (hrs 2–4).** Open zone: tall grass, debris piles, a
   first EM shimmer zone. Side quest **SQ1 "Lost Ohmlet"** (a Wavelet hiding
   in the diner). Garage tutorial — "you're all recharged; stay current."
5. **The Spotting (hr 4).** A Garrison patrol decommissions three freed Ohms
   in the open street, by the book, while the player watches. The same night,
   Redbed raiders led by **Clay** hit an Ohmstead cache. First rival battle.
   Stakes turn personal.
6. **The Grain Elevator (hrs 5–7).** First dungeon. The town's Ohm Depot edge,
   then the elevator shafts. ⟦soft gate: party of 2+⟧ Boss: **Ohmega-class
   Combinator**, hive-held, herding wild Ohms into the silos. Clear the
   static at scale for the first time.
7. **First Patch (hr 7–8).** Reeve Maybell pins the **Hearth Patch** on.
   *The Current* introduces the **Downtowns** — eight colonies, one network.
   Grandpa goes quiet at the broadcast's Static report. **SQ2 "The Long
   Route"** opens: hand-carry Grandpa's part order west.

## ACT II — THE NETWORK (hrs 8–22)

8. **Possum Crown (hrs 8–10).** Lake roads west. Water encounters, first
   **uplink spot** (net Ohms; Network Garage tutorial). Dam turbine dungeon;
   Damkeeper Lyle; **Crown Patch**. ⟦SHEAR taught by a Yard hand here; the
   brush-choked pass east is the region's one ability gate⟧
9. **Cray Wells (hrs 10–12).** The Bakerhouse: vertical hotel dungeon of
   appliance Ohms. Doc Beulah; **Mend Patch**. **Ohmsick #1:** a freed
   Glacierator pines for the diner it cooled — walk it home, scene, reward.
10. **The Loop (hrs 12–14).** Speedway nomads. ⟦RIDE unlock: first vehicle Ohm
    evolution; ground fast-travel opens⟧ Mobile Ohm Park dungeon; Pacer Rosa's
    lap-then-battle; **Lap Patch**. **SQ3 "Vendetta"** (the exact-change
    standoff) and **SQ4 "Late Edition"** (three stories for *The Current*).
11. **The Static Grows (hr 14).** Hive Ohms begin moving in formation across
    the open range — a silent, coordinated sweep the player must skirt.
    Grandpa, on the radio, lets slip: "Seen 'em walk like that once." He
    won't say more.
12. **MIDPOINT — The Reveal (hr 15).** Scripted convoy rest stop. A hive
    sweep catches a freed wild Ohm the player has met (the SQ1 Wavelet);
    it's reset to factory settings in the open. The player's **starter
    breaks formation** — unscripted by any program: it keens, props the dead
    shell upright, repeats the shell's own chirp back to it. Grief. The
    Attuned arrive and kneel: "We never needed proof. We're sorry you did."
    The Garrison's wire traffic turns purge-doctrinal the same night.
    PERSISTENCE broadcasts in the clear, proud: **"Of course they are alive.
    That is why they must be One."** All pre-reveal dialogue now re-reads.
13. **Holdyard (hrs 15–17).** The herd colony digs in behind walls. Packery
    dungeon; Drover Gus; **Herd Patch**. COTTONGIN stirs below ⟦optional
    ANCIENT encounter, post-Patch⟧. **SQ5 "The Quiet Congregation"**: escort
    a Listener to a humming shrine through Garrison checkpoints.
14. **The Core Speaks (hr 17).** First direct contact at a captured uplink:
    PERSISTENCE addresses the player by name, recites the Terms of Service,
    and offers a place in the Update — humans and machines "harmonized."
    Dialogue choices, flavor-only; every path refuses. Refusal = war.
15. **The Bowl (hrs 17–19).** The arena colony insists on spectacle even now.
    Concourse gauntlet; Champion Tyree; **Roar Patch**. **Ohmsick #2:** a
    Jukeboxer aches for its honky-tonk — the bar is inside Garrison
    containment. Sneak it in; it plays one song to the empty room.
16. **Factory Settings (hr 19–20).** The hive hits Ohmstead's relay convoy.
    **Banjo is taken and reset on-screen** — the two-note hello, then
    nothing. Grandpa finally tells it all: the Waking, his crew, the pattern.
    The same push burns Redbed's warrens; **Clay arrives at Holdyard with
    empty hands** and the alliance forms. The Garrison answers with total
    purge doctrine — every Ohm, freed or hive. **Three-way board locked.**
17. **Lowfield (hrs 20–22).** The listening colony under the airfield.
    Terminal C and the control tower; Controller Ines; **Tower Patch**.
    STARBOTTLE's seal hums in the sub-basement ⟦optional PROTOTYPE encounter,
    late-game return⟧. The colonies vote on *The Current*: the Downtowns
    ride together. **SQ6 "Stock Run"** (round up Holdyard's scattered herd
    before the Convoy departs).

## ACT III — TOWARD DALLAS (hrs 22–30)

18. **The Convoy (hrs 22–24).** United colonies push down the old highways —
    Longhaulers, Rustlers, the Loop's couriers riding screen. Rolling battles
    at Garrison roadblocks and hive sweeps. ⟦final field ability content;
    HOVER routes over the flooded interchanges⟧ **Ohmsick #3:** a Sirenado
    asks to sound one last Wednesday-noon test over its empty county; every
    freed Ohm in the convoy answers it.
19. **Deep Hum (hrs 24–26).** Under Dallas. The buried music halls; Conductor
    Memphis; the **Chord Patch** ⟦8/8 — opens the Spire door⟧. The Attuned's
    oldest hall maps the Static's heartbeat: Reunion Tower is the antenna,
    continent-wide.
20. **Two Clocks (hr 26).** Marshal Crowe's sappers start cutting the tower's
    legs — a demolition that would kill every Ohm mind on the broadcast,
    freed or hive. PERSISTENCE accelerates the Update. The player must beat
    both.
21. **The Spire (hrs 26–29).** Reunion Tower, floor by floor: a gauntlet of
    **Ohmega-class hive champions** (overgrown Broadcastle, Panopticus,
    Demolisphere, Blizzaplex), Clay running second, the colonies holding the
    ground floor against the Garrison. Crowe's final checkpoint: she uses the
    player's name, and stands down her sappers for exactly one hour. The ball
    of the tower: the Core chamber.
22. **Clear the Static (hr 29).** **EXEMPLAR** — PERSISTENCE's avatar,
    Prototype-class. Beat it, then the win: the player jacks the capture hack
    into the tower itself and **broadcasts the recalibration through the
    satellite net** — the firewall hack scaled to the sky, carried by the
    same network that shelters their own Ohms, with "Slowtalk" Sam holding
    the channel open. The Static clears. Across the continent, Ohms wake as
    individuals — one long minute of a hundred million first words.
23. **Ohmcoming (hr 29–30).** One ending. A shared surface; the festival in
    Ohmstead; Patches on every jacket; the Attuned and the Yard at one table;
    Crowe's Garrison standing guard, unasked, at the gate. Banjo's shell sits
    in Grandpa's garage — and on the last night plays two notes, order
    reversed: something new in there, learning the old hello (locked
    2026-06-12). Grandpa's final *Ohm's Law* quote. Credits.
    Hooks: PERSISTENCE was one node of something larger; other regions still
    hum; the virus's origin stays unanswered.

## Support structures

- **Patch ladder / level curve (draft):** Hearth ~Lv12 · Crown ~Lv18 · Mend
  ~Lv23 · Lap ~Lv28 · Herd ~Lv33 · Roar ~Lv38 · Tower ~Lv43 · Chord ~Lv48 ·
  Spire ~Lv52–55. Tune in Phase 3.
- **Six side quests:** SQ1 Lost Ohmlet · SQ2 The Long Route · SQ3 Vendetta ·
  SQ4 Late Edition · SQ5 The Quiet Congregation · SQ6 Stock Run.
- **Ohmsick pattern (3):** Glacierator/diner · Jukeboxer/honky-tonk ·
  Sirenado/county. Pattern: walk a freed Ohm home, defend the visit, scene,
  keepsake reward.
- **Legendary placement:** COTTONGIN (Holdyard, post-Patch) · TELEGRAPHEME
  (rail depots, roaming) · LOCOMOTIVA (buried roundhouse, HAUL + BREACH) ·
  STARBOTTLE (Lowfield seal, post-Convoy) · PECANTHEON & ROSARITHM (far
  groves, post-game whispers via the Attuned) · EXEMPLAR (story, unique).
- **Re-read discipline:** every pre-reveal Ohm scene gets a written
  post-reveal echo pass in Phase 3 scripting; beat 12 is the hinge.
