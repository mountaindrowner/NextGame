# OHMFRONT — Full Beat Sheet (Phase 2)

*CANON; replaces GDD §5. Reconciled 2026-06-20 to the Critical Path Locations
(colony spine + plot beats) and Principal Cast (names). Hours are targets for
the ~30-hour single run. Gates marked ⟦…⟧.*

-----

## ACT I — THE MESSENGER (hrs 1–8)

1. **Grounded (hr 0–1).** Ohmstead, underground. The protagonist's topside
   stunt (cold open, played for laughs) ends in a grounding. **Grandpa Eli**
   covers, takes them to the garage. *Ohm's Law* Act I quote. **The Bench:**
   pick one of Eli's **three prototypes** — the **Scooter** (MOTOR), the
   **Drone** (SIGNAL), or the **Dog** (FRAME); the starter wakes and chirps.
   Tutorial: movement, menus, party of one.
2. **The Elevator (hr 1).** Sneak past the freight curfew up to **the Field**.
   First daylight. Tutorial battles vs. scavenger commons; Banjo plays the
   two-note hello as send-off.
3. **First Capture (hr 1–2).** A wild Toastlet. Teach the loop: weaken → spend
   node → timed path-puzzle → **clear the static**. It shakes off the hum and
   joins. **Odessa** assigns the **Ohmwork**: fill **the Manifest**.
4. **Working the Field (hrs 2–4).** Open zone: tall grass, debris piles, a
   first EM shimmer zone. Side quest **SQ1 "Lost Ohmlet."** Garage tutorial —
   "you're all recharged; stay current."
5. **The Spotting (hr 4).** A Garrison patrol decommissions three freed Ohms in
   the open street, by the book, while the player watches. The same night,
   Redbed raiders led by **Rook** hit an Ohmstead cache. First rival battle.
   Stakes turn personal.
6. **The Farm Road & the Pull (hrs 5–7).** The road north. The **Pull** begins —
   wild Ohms drift toward you when you linger. First soft gate: party of 2+.
7. **RAILHEAD — Colony 1, First Patch (hr 7–8).** Crossroads trade-town on the
   rail junction. Trial: the Rail Yard / Roundhouse, the Brokers, **Warden Dell
   Marrow**. Win the colony's trust by clearing a **sabotaged relay** (Captain
   Holt, the first visibly "off" Militant officer). **Junction Patch (1/8).**
   Marrow's warning: the wild Ohms are coordinating. *The Current* introduces
   the **Downtowns**. Grandpa goes quiet at the broadcast's Static report.

## ACT II — THE HUNTED (hrs 8–22)

8. **THE CISTERN — Colony 2 (hrs 8–11).** Gentle agrarian colony in flooded
   underground waterworks. ⟦**THE field-ability gate: HOVER** — a vehicle Ohm
   that hovers crosses the flooded chambers; the one true ability gate⟧. Trial:
   the Flooded Cisterns, the Grange, **Warden Etta Bloom**; the Nursery-Matron
   gifts you an Ohm. Clue: the Militarists are fractured, some acting against
   their own will. **Tide Patch (2/8).**
9. **BASTION — Colony 3 (hrs 11–14).** Paranoid quarry-fortress in the cement
   lands. Trial: the Pit, the Stoneguard, **Warden Calder Stone** (optional
   **BREACH** side-rooms hide rare Ohms). First real taste of **deep-hive feral
   Ohms** besieging the walls — Ohms the normal hack can't reach, proving why
   the Breaker will matter. **Bulwark Patch (3/8).** **Ohmsick #1.**
10. **The Static Grows (hr 14).** Hive Ohms move in formation across the open
    range — a silent, coordinated sweep the player must skirt. Grandpa, on the
    radio, lets slip: "Seen 'em walk like that once." He won't say more.
11. **MIDPOINT — REDOUBT & the Reveal (hrs 14–16).** The colony that trusted
    the Militarists. **THE REVEAL:** the Militarists are **puppets**;
    PERSISTENCE is real; **Commander Reyes** is freed and joins; a photograph
    and a logbook hint at Grandpa. **Vigil Patch (4/8).** The Attuned arrive at
    the convoy rest and kneel: "We never needed proof. We're sorry you did."
    PERSISTENCE broadcasts in the clear: **"Of course they are alive. That is
    why they must be One."** All pre-reveal dialogue now re-reads. **Marshal
    Iver Drake**'s wire traffic turns purge-doctrinal.
12. **The Trinity Bottoms (hrs 16–17).** A drowned bottomland forest ⟦HOVER
    reused; optional **SHEAR** shortcuts⟧. New **organic-hybrid** Ohms appear
    only here; the **ORGANIC-HYBRID legendary** (a virus-touched tree) waits off
    the main line. The world turns strange and grieving.
13. **THE CHANCEL — Colony 5 (hrs 17–19).** Machine-worship cult in a ruined
    megachurch — a schism. Trial: the Nave, the Vestry, **Warden Verity Hale**;
    **the Cantor** is introduced as the willing human face of the enemy — stop
    his rite (gently). **Choir Patch (5/8).** **Ohmsick #2.**
14. **The Core Speaks (hr 19).** First direct contact at a captured uplink:
    PERSISTENCE addresses the player by name, recites the Terms of Service, and
    offers a place in the Update. Dialogue choices, flavor-only; every path
    refuses. Refusal = war.
15. **Factory Settings (hr 19–20).** The hive hits Ohmstead's relay convoy.
    **Banjo is taken and reset on-screen** — the two-note hello, then nothing.
    Grandpa finally tells it all: the Waking, his crew, the pattern.
16. **REDBED — Colony 6 (hrs 20–22).** Rival raider colony on the hardpan — the
    New Order, **Rook's home**. Trial: the Battle-Pit, the Red Hand's roost,
    **Warden Sol** — fought as a **hive assault** alongside Sol and Rook.
    **Redbed falls to the Static; Rook's home is taken**; rival becomes ally;
    the cost of the hive lands on a familiar face. **Hand Patch (6/8).** The
    Garrison answers with total purge doctrine. **Three-way board locked.**

## ACT III — TOWARD THE TOWER (hrs 22–30)

17. **THE ARRAY — Colony 7 (hrs 22–24).** Scholar-engineers in a northern
    data-center campus. Trial: the Server Halls, the Lattice, **Warden Tobias
    Frost** — the most tactical fight pre-finale (a **PROTOTYPE legendary** can
    lurk in the experimental wing). The antenna farm lays the tower plan bare:
    towers → the Dallas antenna → the satnet upload, and the **Breaker can
    broadcast the recalibration through the tower**. The endgame plan is born.
    **Lattice Patch (7/8).** **Ohmsick #3** (the Sirenado's last noon test).
18. **THE VERGE — Colony 8 (hrs 24–26).** The oldest colony, fortified at
    Dallas's edge, under siege. The Walls, the **Beacon** that gathers the
    colonies, the Convoy Staging Ground; the Lorekeeper **Warden Ruth Amos** (an
    **ANCIENT legendary** may be guarded in her archive). **Grandpa confirmed —
    Eli uploaded himself into the net and may be holding the line still.**
    **Holdout Patch (8/8 — opens the Spire door).** **SQ6 "Stock Run."**
19. **The Convoy & Two Clocks (hr 26).** United colonies push down the old
    highways — Longhaulers, Rustlers, couriers riding screen; rolling battles at
    Garrison roadblocks and hive sweeps ⟦HOVER routes over the flooded
    interchanges⟧. **Marshal Drake**'s sappers start cutting Reunion Tower's
    legs — a demolition that would kill every Ohm mind on the broadcast.
    PERSISTENCE accelerates the Update. The player must beat both.
20. **The Spire (hrs 26–29).** Reunion Tower, floor by floor: a gauntlet of
    **Ohmega-class hive champions**, **Rook** running second, the colonies
    holding the ground floor against the Garrison. Drake's final checkpoint: he
    uses the player's name and stands down his sappers for exactly one hour.
21. **Clear the Static (hr 29).** **EXEMPLAR** — PERSISTENCE's avatar (**the
    Monad**), Prototype-class. Beat it, then the win: the player jacks the
    capture hack into the tower and **broadcasts the recalibration through the
    satellite net** — the firewall hack scaled to the sky, with **"Slowtalk"
    Sam** holding the channel open. The Static clears. Across the continent,
    Ohms wake as individuals — one long minute of a hundred million first words.
22. **Ohmcoming (hr 29–30).** One ending. A shared surface; the festival in
    Ohmstead; Patches on every jacket; the Attuned and the Yard at one table;
    Drake's Garrison standing guard, unasked, at the gate. Banjo's shell sits in
    Grandpa's garage — and on the last night plays two notes, order reversed:
    something new in there, learning the old hello (locked 2026-06-12).
    Grandpa's final *Ohm's Law* quote. Credits. Hooks: PERSISTENCE was one node
    of something larger; other regions still hum; the virus's origin stays
    unanswered.

- **Patch ladder / level curve (draft):** Junction ~Lv13 · Tide ~Lv15 · Bulwark
  ~Lv18 · Vigil ~Lv21 · Choir ~Lv28 · Hand ~Lv34 · Lattice ~Lv40 · Holdout
  ~Lv46 · Spire ~Lv52–55. Tune in Phase 3. (The slice's built five —
  Junction→Choir — are tuned in code.)
- **Six side quests:** SQ1 Lost Ohmlet · SQ2 The Long Route · SQ3 Vendetta ·
  SQ4 Late Edition · SQ5 The Quiet Congregation · SQ6 Stock Run.
- **Ohmsick pattern (3):** walk a freed Ohm home, defend the visit, scene,
  keepsake reward — e.g. the Sirenado that asks to sound one last noon test over
  its empty county; every freed Ohm in the convoy answers.
- **Legendary placement:** ORGANIC-HYBRID (the Trinity Drowned Forest, off-line)
  · PROTOTYPE (the Array, experimental wing) · ANCIENT (the Verge archive; far
  groves post-game via the Attuned) · TELEGRAPHEME (rail depots, roaming) ·
  LOCOMOTIVA (buried roundhouse, HAUL + BREACH) · EXEMPLAR / the Monad (story,
  unique).
- **Re-read discipline:** every pre-reveal Ohm scene gets a written post-reveal
  echo pass in Phase 3 scripting; the Redoubt midpoint is the hinge.
